# Community conduct

HumanizerOS welcomes careful disagreement. Contributors may challenge rules, thresholds, examples, and product decisions. Criticism should address evidence and behavior, not a person's identity or ability.

We do not accept harassment, threats, publication of private information, discriminatory language, or deliberate disruption of project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and restrict participation when necessary.

A rule proposal that questions an existing assumption is valuable. A contribution that humiliates the author is not.

Report conduct concerns through a private maintainer channel or GitHub's reporting tools. Reports will be reviewed with the minimum disclosure necessary.
